# OUTLAND — A Calm Walking and Exploration Game

> **Transparency note:** The code for this game was not written by us. The entire codebase was generated by artificial intelligence. Please read the *Notes on Development* section below before evaluating this project.

---

## Notes on Development

To be fully transparent: the code for this game was not written by us. The entire codebase was generated by artificial intelligence. Our role was limited to:

- Providing the original idea and creative direction for the game.
- Defining the gameplay loop and designing the user experience.
- Debugging the AI-generated code and ensuring it functions correctly.
- Continuously updating and expanding the game's content and features.

This project was created primarily as a demonstration of the capabilities and potential of artificial intelligence. It is intended to showcase what AI can achieve in game development when guided by human creativity, rather than to claim authorship of the code.

---

## About the Game

OUTLAND is a relaxing exploration game that offers players a peaceful and enjoyable journey without revealing where their next step will lead. Along the way, players pass through forests, mountains, rivers, villages, and even entire countries, and they can walk and travel across different countries (this section is still being expanded, and the number of countries will continue to grow). They can observe the diverse wildlife of each region. Every journey brings unexpected events.

Players can earn coins through their steps and use them to grow stronger, or perhaps to purchase a vehicle. Who knows — there may be even more interesting things to discover, such as encountering ordinary people and everyday street stories in cities, forests, and deserts.

We are currently working on this game, and once players are able to walk across most parts of the Earth — or traverse it by bicycle — major surprises await them, such as a journey to Mars.

This game has been developed through a collaboration between me and a friend, using and extending AI tools. It features dynamic environments, smooth day-and-night transitions, varied weather, natural sounds, calming music, and animal calls.

Enter a journey that begins with a single step and may end somewhere farther than you ever imagined.

## Features

- 🌲 Dynamic environments: forests, mountains, rivers, villages, deserts, and more
- 🌍 Multiple countries to walk through (continuously expanding)
- 🐾 Diverse wildlife and animals in every region
- 🌗 Smooth day-and-night cycle
- 🌦️ Varied weather system
- 🎵 Natural sounds, calming music, and animal calls
- 🪙 Coin system based on your steps
- 🚲 Vehicles to unlock, including bicycles and cars
- 🚀 A hidden surprise awaiting players who go far enough — a journey to Mars
